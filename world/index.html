A living-paint chronicle

Pulse

The world of Reynard the fox, where the old light still moves.

Reynard, keeper of the glow

Every story needs a heart that moves. Ours is Reynard, a fox of quiet courage and bright curiosity. He walks between the town and the stars, and he carries more than he lets on. He is the mascot of Pulse and the thread that runs through every scene.

The square at dusk

The story begins in the town square. The lamps hum, the cobblestones still hold the day's warmth, and Nikola Tesla steps into the circle of light. He is the one who opens the tale. He speaks of currents unseen, of frequencies that bind all living things, and of a pulse beneath the world that only a few have learned to hear.

Metatron, the scribe

Every word of this chronicle passes through the hand of Metatron, scribe and guide. He records what was, what is, and what is still unfolding. When the path grows dim, his ink glows gold to show the way forward.

The Pulse collection

Pulse is a collection of 42 pieces, painted in the living-oil style of this world. It follows its characters through the seasons of a life:

  • a male child and a female child
  • their grown-up selves
  • their elder selves, worn smooth by time and wisdom

On the horizon: Project X

Beyond the square, other footsteps are gathering. Project X is a planned collection led by a raccoon, joined by two or three more animals of the realm. Its size is pending the final council vote.

Project X is still in planning. Its details may change before anything is announced.
