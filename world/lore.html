As recorded by Metatron

The Lore

What the fox carries, where it comes from, and who waits for him among the stars.

Moon-gold

On quiet nights Reynard climbs to the moon. There, in the silver dust, he gathers monatomic gold, the white powder the old alchemists called ORMUS. It does not shine like coins do. It glows softly, the way a memory glows, and the townsfolk say it remembers the light it came from.

The Flower of Life

Around Reynard's neck hangs an amulet shaped like the Flower of Life, circles woven into circles, the pattern under all patterns. When the moon-gold touches it, the amulet hums, and the pulse of the world grows a little easier to hear.

Sirius and the white fox

Far above, Sirius burns brighter than any star near it. Reynard knows why. His mother watches from there. She comes to him only as a white fox, a figure of pure light. No one has seen her face in any other form. When she appears, the night goes still and the paint itself seems to breathe.

Seven, seven, seven

The scribe marks sevens wherever he finds them. Drop 77 is the first gathering, and it grows in weekly batches toward 777. Each batch is another line in the chronicle.

The townsfolk and the kiosks

The square belongs to its animal townsfolk: merchants, menders, and wanderers, each with a story of their own. Their kiosks line the cobblestones, lantern-lit and fragrant with old wood and herbs. You will find:

  • Amulets, carved and charged under the moon
  • Belts, tooled and bound for long roads
  • Outfits for every season of a life
  • Potions that shimmer in their glass
  • Globes glowing in the color of their tier

The staffs you see leaning in the stalls are for display only. They are part of the scenery, not a tier.

Living paint

Nothing in this world sits still. Everything is painted in living oil: heavy brushstrokes that drift like wind through wheat, deep violet and indigo nights, and gold that catches every edge of light. It is never a cartoon. It is a painting that remembered how to move.
